About

I earn trust the old-fashioned way — then I scale with it

I did not “fall into” construction administration. I chose it because I like systems that survive stress: registers that match reality, stock that reconciles, and a file you can defend in an audit six months later.

That mindset is what accelerated my path: from site administration in January 2022 to supervisory responsibility within about a year — because when you remove friction from documentation, gate logic, and reporting, leadership stops firefighting and starts executing.

Today I operate comfortably in two gears: on the ground with supervisors and crews, and at the desk with payroll, procurement, invoices, and formal correspondence. Oil and gas construction punishes gaps in either mode — so I switch between them without dropping standards.

I also use modern tooling (including AI-assisted drafting) to move faster — but never at the cost of accuracy. If it cannot be explained to a regulator, a client, or a payroll auditor, it does not ship with my name on it.

What I am looking for next: a team that values discipline as a competitive advantage — and a role where my blend of workforce leadership, procurement discipline, and documentation depth can protect schedule and margin. Open to international relocation. References on request.
