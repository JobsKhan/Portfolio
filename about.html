About

From filing cabinets to supervising crews

I started in site administration because I like systems: names in the right register, quantities that match the delivery note, and a paper trail that still makes sense six months later. That foundation turned into responsibility faster than I expected — within about a year I was helping run the human and logistical side of the work, not just recording it.

Today I am comfortable in two modes at once: on your feet with supervisors and workers, and at the desk with spreadsheets, invoices, and formal correspondence. Oil and gas construction is not forgiving of gaps in either place, so I have learned to move between them without dropping detail.

I also use modern tools — including AI-assisted drafting and reporting — as accelerators, not replacements for judgment. The goal is always the same: accurate records, clear language, and decisions that can be defended if someone asks questions three projects from now.

I am open to international relocation and high-pressure, multicultural environments. References are available on request.
